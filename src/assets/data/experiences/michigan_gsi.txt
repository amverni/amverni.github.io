I worked on the staff for EECS 485 - Web Systems as a Graduate Student Instructor (GSI) where I 
taught students introductory web development concepts such as server and client-side dynamic pages, 
web security, and MapReduce.
A new focus for us was adding how to use cloud computing technologies such as AWS for scaling up 
web applications which we taught through hands on labs.
On top of the normal responsiblities of being a GSI such as office hours and helping students with 
class concepts, I was responsible for coordinating logistics for the exams and quizzes which was of particular 
importance as we temporarily moved to a completely remote offering of the course.
