During the summer of 2017, I studied abroad in Spain at the [University of Navarra](https://www.unav.edu/en/home).
I spent the first half at [Tecnun](https://tecnun.unav.edu/), the university's college of engineering in 
San Sebastian where I visited many leading engineering companies in the surrounding area to learn about 
foreign business practices. 
I also took a microrobitics course where I worked with a small group of both Michigan and Tecnun students 
to program an arduino based robot to autonomously navigate out of a maze. 
The second half was spent at the University of Navarra's main campus in Pamplona where I studied Spanish 
language and culture as well as Spain's political issues of the time such as the Catalan independence 
movement and the introduction of ride-hailing services.
