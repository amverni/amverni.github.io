I worked on the Outdoor department’s software team building the UI for smartwatches.
* I developed graphical representations of in-activity metrics for fēnix, MARQ, and Forerunner smartwatch lines. 
This included creating responsive gauges, graphs, etc. to visually represent activity data optimized for low resource 
scenarios. I worked closely with the customer experience team because this feature was critical in the marketing 
campaign for the next generation of outdoor and fitness smartwatches.
* I implemented [Stealth Mode](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=DICH3z8gRR82YcOoyTHpV7) on the Instinct Tactical 
which disabled all wireless communication with the watch (e.g., GPS) in order to comply with military regulations 
while still allowing all on-device features to remain functional.
* I built an app to highlight watch functionality to customers while in demo mode at stores.
